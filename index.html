Performance Highlights
Monthly achievements
  • By the Numbers — 488 cards built, 1,327,494 bets placed, $10,888,376 in handle generated across the full month; Average Per Bet rose to $7.88 (vs. $7.42 in '25, +6.2% YoY) and Average Odds increased to +1,680 (vs. +1,542 in '25, +8.9% YoY)
  • March Madness coverage drove strong PPM performance — 150 cards built (vs. 84 in '25, +78.6% YoY), generating 693K bets and $5.66M handle (+46.9% and +45.2% YoY respectively); PPM bet mix rose to 8.90% of SBK (vs. 5.51% in '25); notably, while US Online SBK Parlay/SGP handle fell -28.8% YoY, PPM handle under the same parameters grew +127.1% YoY; the tournament also served as a testing ground for new markets, with Team to Never Trail gaining popularity in parlays
  • Arkansas launch delivered another new-state uplift consistent with prior state launches — ARK ranked 13th by PPM bets despite being only 21st by overall SBK volume, indicating strong module adoption

Opportunity Areas
Gaps, risks & callouts
  • March Madness drove parlay-heavy mix — the tournament shifted the month's bet composition toward multi-game parlays (ML, spread, Race-to-X) and away from SGPs, which pulled down Metrics 2 & 3 (SGP-specific) despite strong overall module performance; this is expected to normalize in April as MLB and NBA drive higher SGP volume
  • YoY comparisons unreliable — module was not visible to 100% of customers during H2.0 rollout last year, making Q1–Q3 2025 data an inconsistent baseline
  • Reduced legs & odds functionality blocked — load concerns remain the primary technical roadblock; to be investigated in Q2
  • MLB season launch was supported by new partnerships with YES Network, NESN, and former Major Leaguer Anthony Rizzo — these broadcast integrations represent a scalable model for driving module awareness through regional fan bases and star-player audiences, and expanding this approach to additional partners could meaningfully grow reach during peak baseball windows
