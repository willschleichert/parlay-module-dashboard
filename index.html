  • Arkansas launch — AR ranked 13th by PPM bets despite being 21st by overall SBK volume, continuing the trend of increased module adoption from new states and by new customers
  • Push notifications — solid NBA Playoffs SGP engagement in the first round, aided by our introduction of PPM push notifications in late April
  • Cody Brown (@CodyBrownBets) drove 54.3% of all partner bets and 56.3% of all partner handle over Mar–Apr, making him by far the dominant partner contributor. He regularly posts picks outside current module parameters (leg count, odds range), so easing restrictions is both a growth lever and a way to better feature his most popular content
  • MLB season launch was supported by new partnerships with YES Network, NESN, MLB Network, and former Major Leaguer Anthony Rizzo — these broadcast integrations represent an opportunity to drive module awareness through regional fan bases and player audiences
  • Module conversion dip in April is one we're monitoring closely. Two notable headwinds: (1) Masters coverage was limited to mostly overnight windows, as all module-eligible markets went in-play once the first group teed off each day; (2) Lower relevance MLB futures cards appeared in the front of the recommender tab for a prolonged stretch due to a blacklist gap — fix in progress
  • YoY comparisons tricky — module was not visible to 100% of customers during H2.0 rollout last year, making Q1–Q3 2025 data an inconsistent baseline
  • Parlay Hub decommission — Show/No Show experiment (Apr 7–21) found no measurable negative impact on handle or eGGR per user; team now pursuing decommission of the feature
  • Ease legs & odds restrictions — current parameters require 4+ legs and +400–+5,000 odds; Parlay Hub decommission would expedite these improvements. Targeting end of NBA Playoffs and World Cup for delivery
  • SGP+ rollout — internal testing kicked off in April; continuing to resolve issues (image display, FF conflicts, leg rendering) ahead of release
  • MLB futures blacklist fix — resolve stale/repetitive futures cards appearing in MLB recommender tab; fix in progress with engineering
  • Automation exploration — actively identifying opportunities to improve operational efficiency, including card creation and data reporting workflows
  • Leg description truncation — investigating UX improvements to ensure customers can more easily read all legs on a card without text getting cut off
